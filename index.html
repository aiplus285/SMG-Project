Demand–Offer Match — Full Breakdown

Does this configuration's unit mix actually match real demand — proven ฿36M+ corridor absorption and the corporate/expat affordability pool — or is it priced beyond what's proven to sell here? Updates live with unit count, positioning, family housing budget and rental yield.

Method & sources
Composite = (share of your mix priced ฿36M+, i.e. Signature + Estate) × High-Tier Absorption Fit + (share priced under ฿36M, i.e. Family + Corporate-Ready) × Low-Tier Affordability Fit. High-Tier Absorption Fit compares your Signature+Estate unit count to the single largest count ever proven sold at a ฿40M+ average price in this corridor (11 units, Siam Royal View) — the report applies this same ceiling to the combined ฿36M+ band. Low-Tier Affordability Fit is graded per unit against the current corporate/expat housing budget at the current assumed rental yield, not a binary cutoff. Sources: the Benchmark Developments table below (Update 3 report, Section 1) · the Corporate & Expat Rental Demand Check panel · CBRE (Thailand) H2 2024 and Bamboo Routes (REIC-derived) market data, carried over from the original analysis behind this tool and not restated in the Update 3 report's own shorter reference list (see footer). A fifth, smaller-footprint shared-pool tier tested during the report's analysis would have scored a fully "green" match, but was excluded here because it drops below the 5-star, every-unit-has-a-private-pool brief this configuration keeps (see Executive Summary). CBRE's qualitative note that luxury/super-luxury demand remains resilient (Middle East, Japan, Russia buyers) isn't reducible to a number and isn't reflected in the score — treat it as context, not evidence the score already counts.

Waterway on site: a stream or canal (Khlong Takiang) appears to cross the property — check flood risk, drainage, and buildable-area impact before finalizing the site plan.

Ownership structure: confirm how a mixed Thai/international buyer base will be handled — Thai-quota units vs. a company-ownership structure — with Thai counsel.
6
Pricing headroom: with villas in this market currently closing ≈8% below asking, build negotiation room into initial list prices rather than pricing to the top of each tier's range.
