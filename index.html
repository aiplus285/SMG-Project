Method & sources
Composite = (share of your mix priced ฿40M+) × High-Tier Absorption Fit + (share priced under ฿40M) × Low-Tier Demand Fit. High-Tier Absorption Fit compares your ฿40M+ unit count to the single largest count ever proven sold at that price band in this corridor (11 units, Siam Royal View). Low-Tier Demand Fit averages (a) the share of your under-฿40M units reachable by the current corporate/expat housing budget at the current assumed rental yield, and (b) the share priced at/under ฿12M, the ceiling of the proxy covering roughly 80% of general Pattaya-area transactions. Sources: CBRE (Thailand) H2 2024 · Bamboo Routes (REIC-derived) · the Benchmark Developments table below · the Corporate & Expat Rental Demand Check panel. CBRE's qualitative note that luxury/super-luxury demand remains resilient (Middle East, Japan, Russia buyers) isn't reducible to a number and isn't reflected in the score — treat it as context, not evidence the score already counts.
